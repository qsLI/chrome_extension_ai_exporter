🚀 AI Exporter - Your Cross-Platform AI Knowledge Archiving & Asset Management Tool

In the AI era, high-quality conversations are digital assets. AI Exporter is a professional browser extension designed to build a seamless AI knowledge archiving experience for you.

Whether you are conducting academic research, debugging code, or brainstorming daily, with just one click, you can losslessly export deep conversations from 16+ mainstream platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini into beautifully formatted PDF, Word, or clean Markdown formats.

Expand your AI knowledge workflow:
• Efficient Collaboration: Quickly share with colleagues or teams, losslessly conveying AI insights and technical solutions;
• Secondary Generation: Use as high-quality corpus Sources, feeding them to ChatGPT, Gemini, or NotebookLM to start deeper, brand-new conversations;
• Local Knowledge Base: Safely archive conversations locally. Not only is it convenient for review anytime, but it also serves as a local knowledge base for your Local AI Agents (like Claude Code, Cursor/Codex, etc.) to read and retrieve directly;
• Cloud Direct Connection: With just one click, you can even permanently sync them to your Notion workspace.

💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Which AI websites does AI Exporter support?
We primarily focus on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini exports, but we also perfectly support 16+ mainstream AI websites including NotebookLM, DeepSeek, Grok, Perplexity, etc. You can even combine conversations from different models into a single page for a merged export.

2. Will formulas, code, and images be lost during export?
No! We have invested heavily in our parsing engine. Whether it's PDF or Markdown, it can perfectly preserve complex LaTeX math formulas, syntax highlighting for various languages, tables, and mixed text-image layouts from the native webpage.

3. Can I export only part of the conversation?
Yes. You can use the checkbox feature to precisely select the message bubbles you need to export.

4. Will the export easily fail if the conversation is extremely long?
No. We have specifically optimized performance for super-long conversations, ensuring that massive amounts of content can be exported completely without truncation or crashing.

5. Can I only export to PDF?
Mainly PDF and Markdown, but we also support exporting to Word, JSON, Text, and Image formats, and support direct syncing to Notion.

6. Is AI Exporter free?
Most features are completely free! Exporting formats like Markdown, TXT, JSON, and long images is permanently free. PDF export provides 3 free quotas per day, which is enough to meet the daily needs of the vast majority of users.
